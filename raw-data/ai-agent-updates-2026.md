## Synthesis For Slide 4

The key update is not "another chatbot is smarter". The real change is that AI products are becoming agent systems:

1. **Models** are stronger at reasoning, coding, tool use and long-running tasks.
2. **Coding agents** are moving from suggestions to end-to-end task execution.
3. **MCP, connectors and skills** let agents connect to current docs, files, APIs, repos and business tools.
4. **Enterprise governance** is becoming central: what can an agent access, what can it execute, where are logs, and when must a human approve?
