# Proposal: Presenter-Paced GSAP Fragments
Generated by: cm-brainstorm-idea
Date: 2026-05-27

## Why We're Doing This

The deck already has a visual system and a centralized GSAP motion controller, but many slides still reveal too much content immediately. That makes the presenter talk against a fully visible slide instead of building the point step by step.

### Qualified Problem

**For:** Sea technical/operations team audience and the presenter.  
**Who:** need a practical briefing that follows the speaking rhythm.  
**The:** current Reveal.js deck is visually polished but uneven in reveal pacing.  
**That:** should progressively disclose each major idea so the audience follows the story.  
**Unlike:** auto-playing cinematic motion that can outrun the presenter.  
**Our approach:** use Reveal fragments for click control and GSAP for short, purposeful motion on each reveal.

### 9 Windows Summary

| Level | Past | Present | Future |
|---|---|---|---|
| Super-system | Slide decks often showed full content at once. | Internal sharing needs demo-like pacing and evidence. | Briefings should feel operated live, not read from a poster. |

| Sub-system | Fragments were hand-authored on selected slides. | `createDeckMotion()` already centralizes GSAP. | Reuse the controller and add small fragment-specific animations. |

## Options Evaluated

### Option A: CSS-Only Fragment Pass

Add `fragment` classes to all cards and rely on Reveal default CSS.

**Effort:** S  
**Risk:** Low  
**Trade-off:** Fast but generic; weak illustration value.

### Option B: Cinematic Auto-Play Per Slide

Build GSAP timelines that automatically reveal slide content after entering each slide.

**Effort:** M/L  
**Risk:** Medium  
**Trade-off:** Looks polished but can break presenter timing and speaker notes.

### Option C: Presenter-Paced Fragments + GSAP Micro-Illustrations

Reveal content only on presenter click, then use GSAP to animate that fragment with contextual motion.

**Effort:** M  
**Risk:** Low/Medium  
**Trade-off:** Best balance of control, clarity and polish.

## Recommendation

Choose **Option C**.

1. It keeps the presenter in control through Reveal fragments.
2. It reuses the existing GSAP controller instead of adding a competing motion system.
3. It supports the deck's Operator Briefing System: calm, technical, evidence-led and easy to rehearse.

## Implementation Scope

1. Add fragment sequencing to slides that currently reveal too much at once: Adam, agenda, chatbot/agent bridge, common agents, prompt, skill map/reference slides, workflow, guardrails, demo proof, paradox and Q&A.
2. Keep existing fragment behavior on Survey, AI Trend and Demo Journey.
3. Extend `script.js` with contextual fragment animations for workflow runner, prompt tiles, skill references, proof media and paradox mechanisms.
4. Preserve reduced-motion fallback and avoid layout-shifting animation.
