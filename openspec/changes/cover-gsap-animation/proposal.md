# Proposal: Cover GSAP Animation
Generated by: cm-brainstorm-idea
Date: 2026-05-26

## Why we're doing this

The title cover is the first audience impression of the Operator Briefing System. It should feel alive and technical without becoming a demo of animation for its own sake.

## Options Evaluated

### Option A: Cover Entrance Choreography

Sequence the brand, nav, title, subtitle, demo pill, metadata and workflow diagram into view.

- Effort: Small
- Risk: Low
- Value: Strong opening rhythm with little maintenance cost

### Option B: Workflow Diagram Pulse

Keep the current diagram static, then use a subtle loop to keep the active `Reason / Plan` step alive.

- Effort: Small
- Risk: Low
- Value: Reinforces the agentic coding workflow without adding new UI

### Option C: Ambient Scan/Grid Motion

Add continuous grid, scanline or background motion across the cover.

- Effort: Medium
- Risk: Medium
- Value: More visual energy, but higher distraction risk during the opening talk

## Recommendation

Choose Option A plus Option B.

This gives the cover a polished opening cue and a small ongoing signal on the active workflow step, while preserving the calm internal-briefing tone. Option C is intentionally deferred because the deck already has particles and a dark command-center background.
